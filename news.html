    Perspective on DNA-Compatible On-DNA Chemistry for DNA-Encoded Libraries Published in Bioconjugate Chemistry

    In this Perspective in Bioconjugate Chemistry (2026), Prof. Xiaojie Lu of SIMM reviews the development of DNA-compatible (on-DNA) reactions for DNA-encoded library (DEL) technology. He emphasizes that the value of a DEL depends on the quality, structural diversity and bio-relevance of the chemical space it can access rather than library size alone, and discusses scaffold-generating on-DNA reactions as well as emerging photochemical, electrochemical and biocatalytic tools. The article calls for a shift toward function-oriented, prediction-driven DEL chemistry built on reproducibility, scalability, substrate generality and medicinal-chemistry relevance. DOI: 10.1021/acs.bioconjchem.6c00344

  • 202026-04

    Review on DNA-Encoded Libraries for E3 Ligase Ligand Discovery Published in ChemMedChem

    Our group has published a review in ChemMedChem on the use of DNA-encoded libraries (DELs) to discover ligands for E3 ubiquitin ligases—key components of targeted protein degradation strategies such as PROTACs. The article surveys advances in non-covalent E3 ligase ligand discovery, hit-to-lead optimization and degrader design. It also discusses covalent DELs, DNA-encoded cyclic peptide libraries and direct screening of targeted protein degradation molecules as emerging directions. Lulu Wen and Qingqing Zhang contributed equally to the review, with Rui Jin and Xiaojie Lu as corresponding authors. Read the article (DOI: 10.1002/cmdc.202501032).
