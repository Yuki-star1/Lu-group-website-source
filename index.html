RESEARCH View All Research →

Our research focuses on challenging intracellular targets, orally active cyclic peptide therapeutics, and targeted delivery systems for small molecules and cyclic peptides. We combine covalent chemistry, peptide design, bifunctional modalities, and ligand discovery to develop drug candidates with improved selectivity, delivery, and therapeutic potential.

  • 012026-08

    Perspective on DNA-Compatible On-DNA Chemistry for DNA-Encoded Libraries Published in Bioconjugate Chemistry

    Prof. Xiaojie Lu (SIMM) has published a Perspective in Bioconjugate Chemistry (2026) reviewing DNA-compatible on-DNA chemistry for DNA-encoded libraries (DEL) — from scaffold-generating reactions to emerging photochemical, electrochemical and biocatalytic tools — and calls for a function-oriented, prediction-driven DEL chemistry that reaches biologically relevant chemical space.

  • 112026-05

    Congratulations to Xudong Wang, Zijian Liu, and Guoliang Wang on Successfully Defending Their Theses

    Our group congratulates Xudong Wang, Zijian Liu, and Guoliang Wang on their successful degree thesis defenses and wishes them continued success.

  • 202026-04

    Review on DNA-Encoded Libraries for E3 Ligase Ligand Discovery Published in ChemMedChem

    Our group reviews how DNA-encoded libraries can uncover E3 ligase ligands for targeted protein degradation, from non-covalent hits and their optimization to emerging covalent, cyclic peptide and direct degrader-screening strategies.
