Research Interests

Development of drug discovery technology based on DNA-encoded chemical libraries (DEL). By integrating DEL screening with other drug discovery technologies — such as AI-powered drug design, chemical proteomics, and natural product-based drug development — the group designs and synthesizes focused DNA-encoded libraries against specific targets to accelerate the development of small-molecule, cyclic peptide, and nucleic acid drugs.

  • Design, synthesis and screening of focused DNA-encoded libraries for specific targets
  • Design, synthesis and screening technology development of DNA-encoded cyclic peptide libraries, sugar libraries and covalent compound libraries
  • Development of targeted delivery technology for nucleic acid drugs
  • Application of DNA-encoded libraries in the development of protein degradation drugs (molecular glue and PROTACs)
