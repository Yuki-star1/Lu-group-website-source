Recruitment

We welcome applications from motivated postdoctoral researchers, Ph.D. students, and M.S. students. Undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience are also encouraged to contact us.

Our group focuses on DNA-encoded library technologies and their applications in drug discovery. We are particularly interested in candidates who:

  • Have a strong interest in scientific research and a solid foundation in chemistry, biology, pharmaceutical sciences, or a related discipline;
  • Are curious, responsible, and eager to work in a collaborative and interdisciplinary research environment;
  • Are interested in applying chemical and biological approaches to important questions in drug discovery.

Applicants from interdisciplinary backgrounds, including chemistry, biology, pharmaceutical sciences, and computational science, are encouraged to apply.

To inquire about available opportunities, please email your CV and a brief statement of research interests to xjlu@simm.ac.cn. Please use the subject line “Application – Position – Name – Current Institution.”

Prospective graduate students should also consult the latest admissions information and application requirements published by the relevant graduate school.
